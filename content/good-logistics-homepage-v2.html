Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Before Cargo Moves

Can you collect cargo from multiple suppliers?

Yes. Share each pickup location, cargo-ready date and packing list. We can plan one combined shipment or staged releases where cargo is compatible.

Can you apply FNSKU and outer-carton labels?

Yes. We apply customer-approved label files and placement instructions. Platform requirements must be checked for the destination market.

Is receiving the same as a product-quality inspection?

No. Receiving checks cover quantity, outer cartons, labels and visible damage. Product-quality inspection needs agreed sampling, criteria and reporting.

Can you store and release cargo in stages?

Yes. Share the expected storage period, cargo details and release schedule. Availability and handling are quoted for the shipment.

Do you handle oversized cargo and U.S. island destinations?

Yes. Send packed dimensions, weight, ZIP code and unloading or access needs. We will identify available routing and equipment for the address.
