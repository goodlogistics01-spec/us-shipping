Frequently Asked Questions

Questions to Settle Before Cargo Moves

Can you collect cargo from multiple suppliers?

Yes. Supplier locations, cargo-ready dates, packing lists and release instructions are reviewed before a combined pickup and consolidation plan is confirmed.

Can you apply FNSKU and outer-carton labels?

Yes. Labeling can be arranged using the final files and placement instructions confirmed by the customer. Current platform requirements still need shipment-specific verification.

Is receiving the same as a product-quality inspection?

No. Standard receiving can cover package quantity, outer cartons, labels and visible damage. Product checks, sampling and acceptance criteria must be scoped separately.

Can you store and release cargo in stages?

Temporary storage and planned releases can be coordinated after the cargo, expected duration, inventory record and release instructions are confirmed.

Do you handle oversized cargo and U.S. island destinations?

Yes, subject to review of packed cargo data, routing, equipment, access and final-delivery conditions for the confirmed address.
