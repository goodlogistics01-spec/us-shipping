Frequently Asked Questions

Questions to Settle Before Booking

Clear inputs produce a clearer route and quotation scope.

How much does shipping from China to the USA cost?

Cost depends on the commodity, package count, dimensions, weight, origin, destination ZIP code, shipping method, customs scope and delivery conditions.

How long does sea freight from China to the USA take?

The complete timeline depends on pickup, sailing schedule, port pair, customs and final delivery. Request a current route-specific estimate rather than relying on a fixed promise.

Do you provide DDP door-to-door shipping?

DDP can be reviewed when the commodity, value, importer requirements and exact delivery scope are confirmed.

Can you collect cargo from multiple suppliers?

Yes. Supplier locations, cargo-ready dates, packages and documents are reviewed before confirming a consolidation plan.

Can you ship oversized machinery or furniture?

Oversized cargo can be planned after exact packed dimensions, weights, handling photos, access and unloading requirements are provided.

Do you deliver to Amazon FBA warehouses?

Amazon FBA delivery coordination is available when the assigned destination, shipment plan and carton or label scope are confirmed.

Can you ship to Hawaii, Alaska and Puerto Rico?

These destinations can be reviewed. The final route, island or remote-area handoff and delivery requirements must be confirmed in the quote.

What information is required for a shipping quote?

Send the product, package count, dimensions, total weight, pickup city, U.S. ZIP code, cargo-ready date and any FBA, residential or oversized requirements.
