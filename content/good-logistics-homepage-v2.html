Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Request a Quote

Can you collect cargo from multiple suppliers?

Yes. Send each pickup location, ready date and packing list. We can plan one shipment or staged releases for compatible cargo.

Are receiving checks the same as product inspection?

No. Receiving checks cover agreed package quantities, outer cartons, labels and visible damage. Product inspection needs a separate checklist, sampling method and report.

Which destinations can you quote?

We quote shipments from China to the USA, Canada, the UK, Europe, Australia and New Zealand. U.S. planning also includes Hawaii, Alaska and Puerto Rico.

What should I send first?

Send the product, pickup location, delivery address and any known weight or dimensions. Unknown details can be confirmed with you.
