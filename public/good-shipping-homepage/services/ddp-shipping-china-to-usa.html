Service process

1. China Pickup

We coordinate supplier pickup across major manufacturing regions in China, confirm cargo details and packaging, and arrange export preparation before departure.

2. International Transit

We recommend the freight method based on cargo size, urgency, budget and destination requirements, then coordinate booking and status follow-up.

3. US Delivery

Customs-clearance coordination is handled through licensed brokers or partners, while final-delivery options are confirmed for the destination address and access conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What should a DDP quote include?

A clear DDP quote should define origin pickup, export handling, international freight, licensed-broker customs responsibilities, duties and taxes scope, and the confirmed final-delivery address.

Is DDP always the cheapest option?

Not always. DDP can simplify coordination, but the included scope must be compared carefully against standard freight options.

Can DDP be used for Amazon FBA?

DDP scope for an FBA shipment can be reviewed when carton, labeling, appointment and delivery requirements are confirmed before booking.
