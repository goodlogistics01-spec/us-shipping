Amazon FBA Shipping from China to the USA

FBA freight coordination from Chinese suppliers to the assigned U.S. destination, with preparation and appointment scope confirmed against the seller’s shipment plan.

Get a Quote

When this service fits

  • Amazon sellers shipping inventory from Chinese suppliers
  • Shipments requiring carton marks and delivery appointments
  • LCL, FCL, air freight or scope-defined DDP options for assigned U.S. FBA destinations
  • Sellers consolidating cargo from multiple Chinese suppliers

Service process

1. China Pickup

We coordinate supplier pickup across major manufacturing regions in China, confirm cargo details and packaging, and arrange export preparation before departure.

2. International Transit

We recommend the freight method based on cargo size, urgency, budget and destination requirements, then coordinate booking and status follow-up.

3. US Delivery

Customs-clearance coordination is handled through licensed brokers or partners, while final-delivery options are confirmed for the destination address and access conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Can you deliver directly to Amazon FBA?

FBA delivery can be coordinated for the assigned destination after the shipment plan, labels, carrier requirements and appointment scope are confirmed.

Can you consolidate inventory from multiple suppliers?

Pickup and consolidation can be coordinated after supplier locations, cargo-ready dates and the agreed preparation scope are confirmed.

What FBA details are required?

Provide shipment IDs, carton labels, destination warehouse, carton count, dimensions, weight and any Amazon receiving instructions.

Ready to discuss your shipment?

Tell us your product, supplier city, destination and timing requirements. Good Logistics will review a shipment-specific plan and quotation scope.
