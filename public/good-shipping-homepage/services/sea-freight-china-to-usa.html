Service process

1. China Pickup

We coordinate supplier pickup across major manufacturing regions in China, confirm cargo details and packaging, and arrange export preparation before departure.

2. International Transit

We recommend the freight method based on cargo size, urgency, budget and destination requirements, then coordinate booking and status follow-up.

3. US Delivery

Customs-clearance coordination is handled through licensed brokers or partners, while final-delivery options are confirmed for the destination address and access conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose FCL or LCL sea freight?

FCL is usually better for larger shipments or cargo needing container control. LCL is suitable for smaller shipments that can share container space.

Can sea freight include final delivery?

A quotation can review ocean freight, customs-clearance coordination through licensed brokers or partners, and truck-delivery options after the address and access conditions are provided.

What affects the sea freight price?

Origin city, port pair, cargo volume, weight, commodity, delivery ZIP code, liftgate needs, oversized handling and customs scope all affect the quote.
