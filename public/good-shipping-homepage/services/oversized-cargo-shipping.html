Shipment planning for heavy, oversized, fragile or high-value cargo, including packaging, loading and partner-carrier delivery requirements.

When this service fits

  • Furniture, massage chairs, machinery and industrial equipment
  • Stage lighting, professional audio and high-value project cargo
  • Cargo requiring custom packaging, loading or liftgate planning
  • Shipment options for mainland USA, Hawaii, Alaska or Puerto Rico, subject to route and address confirmation

Service process

1. China Pickup

We coordinate supplier pickup across major manufacturing regions in China, confirm cargo details and packaging, and arrange export preparation before departure.

2. International Transit

We recommend the freight method based on cargo size, urgency, budget and destination requirements, then coordinate booking and status follow-up.

3. US Delivery

Customs-clearance coordination is handled through licensed brokers or partners, while final-delivery options are confirmed for the destination address and access conditions.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as oversized cargo?

Cargo may be oversized when dimensions, weight, packaging or delivery access require special handling beyond standard parcel or pallet freight.

Can you handle oversized cargo to U.S. islands?

Oversized-cargo options for Hawaii, Alaska and Puerto Rico are reviewed case by case after dimensions, weight, route and local-handoff requirements are confirmed.

What information is needed before booking?

Send product photos, packed dimensions, gross weight, supplier city, loading method and final delivery address details.

Ready to discuss your shipment?

Tell us your product, supplier city, destination and timing requirements. Good Logistics will review a shipment-specific plan and quotation scope.
