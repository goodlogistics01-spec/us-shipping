Frequently asked questions

How does shipping from China to Alaska work?

Pickup can be coordinated across major manufacturing regions in China before ocean or air movement to a U.S. gateway and onward coordination to the confirmed Alaska address.

Can you deliver to remote Alaska destinations?

Door-delivery options are reviewed for the exact Alaska address; remote-area and local-handoff requirements must be confirmed before quoting.

Can you handle oversized cargo to Alaska?

Yes. We review dimensions, weight, loading method, packaging and destination access before building an Alaska oversized cargo plan.
