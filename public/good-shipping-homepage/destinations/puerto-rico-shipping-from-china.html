Frequently asked questions

Does Puerto Rico use U.S. customs rules?

Puerto Rico shipments require U.S. import documentation. The commercial invoice, packing list, commodity details and licensed-broker responsibilities should be confirmed for each shipment.

Can you ship door-to-door from China to San Juan?

Door-delivery options can be reviewed for San Juan and other confirmed Puerto Rico addresses after route, licensed-broker customs and local-handoff requirements are defined.

What details are needed for Puerto Rico delivery planning?

Provide the product, package dimensions and weight, supplier city, consignee details, final address and any access or appointment requirements.

Ready to ship from China to Puerto Rico?

Send cargo dimensions, supplier city, destination address and timeline. We will review the best route and quote scope.
