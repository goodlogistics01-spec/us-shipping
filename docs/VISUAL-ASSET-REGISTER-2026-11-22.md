## Public-use boundary

The photograph may illustrate the type of international container-freight environment in which a shipment moves. It must not be presented as:

- a port, terminal, container, vehicle, facility, or equipment owned or operated by Good Logistics;
- a Good Logistics shipment, customer, route, delivery, or case study;
- proof of a specific carrier relationship, port service, transit time, or geographic coverage;
- evidence of warehouse ownership, fleet ownership, cargo volume, or company scale.

Captions and surrounding copy therefore remain general. The photograph provides industry context rather than operational proof of a GOOD LOGISTICS facility, fleet or specific shipment.
