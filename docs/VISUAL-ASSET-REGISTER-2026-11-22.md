## Selection and crop basis

The replacement was selected because it has an open composition, calm natural blue-grey water and sky, and a clearly visible container ship without the previous port-name signage or a road vehicle dominating the foreground. It is stock industry photography, not company operational evidence.

All derivatives were cropped and compressed with Sharp from the local working source. No generative editing, retouching, object replacement, or colour remapping was applied, and source metadata was not retained.

- Desktop: centred 16:9 crop from the 2400 × 1800 working source, then resized to 960 × 540, 1600 × 900 and 2400 × 1350.
- Mobile: centred 8:5 crop from the 2400 × 1800 working source, then resized to 640 × 400 and 960 × 600.
- Desktop and mobile rendered crops must be checked again on the final local build before release.

## Public-use boundary

The photograph may illustrate the international ocean-freight setting in which cargo moves. It must not be presented as:

- a vessel, container, cargo, facility, or equipment owned or operated by Good Logistics;
- a Good Logistics shipment, customer, route, delivery, or case study;
- proof of a specific carrier relationship, sailing, transit time, port service, or geographic coverage;
- evidence of fleet ownership, cargo volume, company scale, or service performance.
